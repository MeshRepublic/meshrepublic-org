The premise

Every top-down identity system eventually serves whoever sits at the top. The Mesh Republic inverts the stack: identity begins at the self, each citizen is their own certificate authority, money is minted from personhood rather than capital, and the whole record is witnessed by Bitcoin, the hardest ledger humans have built. The square to the left is the Mandelbrot set, drawn by your browser just now from one iterated rule; infinite unique structure from a single simple law is the entire thesis, and it is why the Republic's currency carries the name.
